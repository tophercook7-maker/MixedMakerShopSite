# Website Cost Hot Springs Small Business: Starter vs Full Setup

## The Basics of a Small‑Business Site

I’ve been helping folks in Hot Springs and the surrounding areas build websites for over twenty years. The first thing most people ask is, “What’s the website cost Hot Springs small business?” The answer depends on how many features you want and how much custom work you need.

## $400 Starter Site

A $400 starter site is a solid choice if you just need a few pages: Home, About, Services, and Contact. It’s built on a template, so the design is clean but not fully customized. You’ll get basic styling, a responsive layout that works on phones and tablets, and a simple contact form. Hosting is included for the first year, and I’ll set it up so you can log in and make small edits.

## $900 Full Setup

If you want a more polished look, more pages, or advanced functionality, the $900 full setup is the way to go. That price covers custom design work, a few extra pages, a more sophisticated contact system, and basic local SEO tweaks. I’ll also add a Google Business Profile link, so your business shows up in local search results. The site will still be hosted for the first year, and I’ll walk you through the admin panel so you can keep it up to date.

## What Drives the Cost Up

The difference between the starter and full setups isn’t just the price tag. It’s about the amount of time I spend on each site and the features I add. Custom graphics, more detailed page layouts, and extra scripts for things like appointment booking or a photo gallery all add to the time and skill required. Local SEO—making sure your site is optimized for the Hot Springs area, adding your address, phone number, and opening hours—also costs time, and I include that in the full setup.

## Why the Free Preview Comes First

Before I start building, I give you a free preview of how your pages will look. This lets you see the layout, suggest changes, and make sure the design feels right. It saves us both time; you won’t have to deal with a site that looks off or has pages you didn’t want. Once you approve the preview, I move on to the final build.

## The Process in Plain Talk

1. **Talk** – I’ll ask you what you need: how many pages, what kind of look, and any special functions.  
2. **Preview** – I’ll send you a mockup of the layout.  
3. **Build** – I’ll code the pages, add the content you give me, and make sure everything works.  
4. **Test** – I’ll check the site on a phone, tablet, and desktop.  
5. **Launch** – I’ll upload it to the host, set up the domain, and point it to you.  
6. **Hand‑off** – I’ll show you how to log in and make simple edits.

I keep the process short and simple because I know small‑business owners don’t want to get lost in tech jargon.

A website that looks good but doesn’t show up in local search is like a shop with no sign.
